<objective>
Rebuild `/deklaracja-dostepnosci` from a 56-line stub into the full statutory declaration.

Purpose: this is a legally required document for a public body, separately fineable when missing (up
to 5 000 zł) or persistently non-conformant (up to 10 000 zł), and since version 2.0 of the Warunki
techniczne, binding on every declaration prepared from 1 August 2024, it is a MACHINE-READABLE
document rather than merely prose. A declaration with perfect Polish and no identifier attributes is
technically non-conformant. This site's declaration is being authored in August 2026, so v2.0 applies
unconditionally and there is no transitional option.

**After this plan a citizen can do something they could not do before**: read what this site claims
about its own accessibility, and find out exactly how to demand better.

Output: the store, the reader, the copy module, the rebuilt page, and the acceptance suite for a route
that has ZERO test coverage today.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| editor-writable store to public page | leaf values travel into a JSON file, into a commit on a public repository, through a build-time reader, onto a page a citizen reads to learn how to file an accessibility complaint |
| repository content to regulatory artefact | this page is the legal statement of this site's conformance |
