<objective>
Finish the high-contrast register at route level, then prove the whole mode with two complementary
automated checks.

Purpose: six route stylesheets carry their own section bands, shadows and tint-only edges, and the
override register says outright that the plan must SWEEP them rather than assume none exist. Once
they are done the mode is complete, and it can be proven. Proof needs two instruments, not one: axe
for the standard AA rules, and a property sweep for the failure this specific mechanism produces,
because axe reports an INDETERMINATE background as `incomplete` rather than as a violation, and the
pass most likely to be wrong is the one least likely to fail.

Output: the route-level overrides and `tests/kontrast.spec.ts`, the first spec in this repository to
assert on anything other than violations.
</objective>
