<objective>
Give the deklaracja an editor. This is the second half of the A11Y-03 slice: plan 09 let a citizen
read the declaration, this plan lets a staff member maintain it.

Purpose: D-14's driver is a recurring legal obligation, not convenience. The declaration must carry a
review date and be reviewed ANNUALLY. A developer-owned module means a developer every year forever;
a panel screen means the staff member who performs the review is the one who records it. Adding a
panel screen is cheap and safe on this project specifically, because the on-disk route walk turns a
missing Polish-sweep entry from a silent gap into a red test.

**After this plan a real editor can do something they could not do before**: perform the annual
review and publish it, unaided, in Polish, with no developer and no JavaScript.

Output: the validator, the screen, the five enumeration-surface entries and the staff-manual section
that states the annual obligation in plain Polish.
</objective>
