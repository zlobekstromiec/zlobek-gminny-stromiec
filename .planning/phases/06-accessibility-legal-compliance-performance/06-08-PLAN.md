<objective>
Build the mandatory EU-funding marking strip, with placeholder logotypes, on every route.

Purpose: the source data document requires a logo row (Fundusze Europejskie, barwy RP, Unia
Europejska, Krajowy Plan Odbudowy) plus the klauzula „Dofinansowane przez Unię Europejską –
NextGenerationEU". The logotype files, the exact amount and the project period are all outstanding
client inputs. D-17 therefore designs and builds the strip NOW, behind launch-gate markers, so that
it gets a design-contract amendment, an axe pass and a high-contrast register row like every other
surface, instead of being pasted in at launch on the day nobody has time to check it.

**This deliverable has no requirement ID.** The user deliberately chose to proceed without minting
one, so it is tracked by decision in `06-CONTEXT.md` and in the ROADMAP rather than in the
requirements traceability table. The requirement IDs on this plan are the ones the new surface must
not break.

Output: the strip, its content module, the copy-sweep exemption, and the tests that pin all three.
</objective>

    The klauzula contains an EN DASH and it is kept. That en dash is part of the mandated wording, and

    carry a line comment, and the strict form is what D-19 defines.

    (b) The strip in `Footer.svelte`, below the four columns and above the copyright, on every route.
    It is a `<section>` with an accessible name and it is NOT aria-hidden. That is the difference
    between it and the decorative logo slots sitting a few lines above it: those repeat branding, this
    is a legal artefact and it is announced.

    Plate: the surface token, small radius, 12px block padding and 16px inline padding, 24px block
    margin. The white plate is the brand lockup precedent and it exists for the same reason: the four
    logotypes are multi-colour marks with their own brand rules and none of them is legible directly
    on the brand-blue footer.

    Logo row: a wrapping, centred flex list with 24px gaps and a LOCKED 48px item height with automatic
    width. The shared height is what structurally enforces the programme's own rule that the EU emblem
    and the Fundusze Europejskie mark are never rendered smaller than any other mark in the row. Do
    not express that rule as a comment and per-item sizes.

    Phase 6 chips: four boxes at the locked height, minimum 96px wide, warm surface, 2px strong border,
    small radius, caption in the label type step and the muted token, which measures 7.58 to 1 on the
    warm surface. Captions from the content module.

    Klauzula: the 15px body step at weight 700, the ink token on the plate (14.65 to 1), centred below
    the row with 12px of top margin.

    (c) „Aktywny Maluch" MOVES here. It currently sits in the brand column as an aria-hidden program
    slot; it is part of the funding marking, so it belongs in this strip, and the brand column keeps
    only „Herb gminy". This supersedes one line of the Phase 1 contract and the Amendment v1.8 block

    (d) High contrast: add the strip's rows to the Footer's existing override block. The plate KEEPS
    white and gains a 2px border in the text value (image plate, D-10); the klauzula and every chip
    caption are pinned to the background value, because the text value on the white plate measures
    1.07 to 1 and is banned outright.

    (e) Performance: the strip ships NO image in this phase, so it adds no request. When the real
    assets land in Phase 7 they become enhanced images with explicit width and height and a literal
    source with width descriptors, never the enhanced-import form, because that form emits density
    descriptors and browsers then ignore the sizes attribute (the 04-02 ruling). Four unsized images
    in the footer of every route would be the single largest layout-shift regression available to this
    phase, so state the constraint in the component comment where Phase 7 will read it.

    re-baseline points, authorised by UI-SPEC Contract 9. Review the diff first: the only change on
