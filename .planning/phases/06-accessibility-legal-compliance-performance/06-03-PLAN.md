<objective>
Land the text-scale mechanism: one multiplier, fourteen type tokens, and a mechanical token-for-token
substitution across the 167 public font-size declarations.

Purpose: D-03 gives visitors three text-size steps (normal, 115 percent, 130 percent). The mechanism
must cost nothing when the multiplier is 1, which is what lets the phase claim the default theme
cannot regress as a property of the mechanism rather than of anyone's care. This plan lands the
mechanism inert: nothing sets the attribute yet, so the visual baseline from plan 01 must stay green,
and that green is the proof that 167 rewritten declarations changed no rendered pixel.

Output: the `:root` token block, the global visually-hidden utility, 32 converted files, four
declared layout fixes, and the two test surfaces that make 130 percent visible to CI.
</objective>
