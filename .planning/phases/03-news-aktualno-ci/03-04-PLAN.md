## Phase Goal

**As a** parent, **I want to** read the żłobek's latest news, with the newest posts also surfaced on the homepage, **so that** I stay informed about events and announcements from my child's żłobek.

**Vertical slice:** after this plan the homepage surfaces the three newest posts (it showed nothing before, only the empty stub). This closes the loop so a parent landing on the homepage immediately sees the latest news and can jump into a post.
