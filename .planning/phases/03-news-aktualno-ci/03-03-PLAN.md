## Phase Goal

**As a** parent, **I want to** read the żłobek's latest news, with the newest posts also surfaced on the homepage, **so that** I stay informed about events and announcements from my child's żłobek.

## MVP Slice (this plan)

**Vertical slice:** after this plan, żłobek staff can create, edit, and publish a news post through the CMS in Polish, without a developer, and it goes live after the rebuild (NEWS-03). This is the staff-facing capability that feeds the visitor routes.

<objective>
Deliver the Sveltia aktualnosci folder collection (NEWS-03) with a Polish copy contract and a publication-date-prefixed slug, plus the staff instrukcja section for the news-publishing flow.

Purpose: Let staff self-publish news posts that the build reader (Plan 01) and routes (Plans 01/02) turn into live pages.
Output: A validated aktualnosci collection in config.yml (all-Polish labels/hints, constrained markdown, inherited image pipeline, date-prefixed slug) and a Polish news section in the instrukcja, with a live human-verified publish loop at end of phase.
</objective>
