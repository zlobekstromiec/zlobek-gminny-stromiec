<objective>
Build `/admin/galeria`: one screen holding the whole photo list, one „Zapisz".

Purpose: GALLERY-02's staff half, and the store the public gallery section reads in plan
05-07. Twelve photos in one sitting is one commit and one Cloudflare build; a screen per
photo would be twelve commits and twelve builds against a free ceiling of 500 per month.

Output: the gallery store, its reader, the `galeria-` naming and ownership pair, the
validator, the screen, and the twelve cap.

## Slice capability (MVP)

ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 3, in part, verbatim: *"Staff can add and remove gallery
photos ... via the CMS."*

After task 2 an editor can open Galeria from the panel navigation, attach a photo, caption
it, describe it, reorder it and save.

## The interval this plan opens, stated plainly

Between this plan and plan 05-07 the panel briefly carries two photo screens: `/admin/o-nas`
still owns the facility photos that `/o-nas` renders, and `/admin/galeria` writes a store the
public page does not read yet. That is one wave inside one phase execution, and it is the
lesser of the two available intermediate states: the alternative ordering removes photo
editing entirely for the same interval.

**Do not invite an editor into the panel between plan 05-06 and plan 05-07, and do not run
any live UAT until plan 05-08 has landed.** The phase's live UAT is scheduled once, at the
end, at a budget of three saves and one rebuild wait.

  `05-CONTEXT.md` §Sequencing corrects it. No "push first" step.

  05 D-11). One atomic commit per save, as the org-owned GitHub App.
