<objective>
Make `/cennik` reachable and make the footer's dead shortcuts land somewhere real.

Purpose: a fees page nobody can navigate to is not a capability. This plan also fixes the
trap `05-RESEARCH.md` C-4 names: `#dojazd` does not exist anywhere in the repository, so
D-17's repoint would ship a fragment that lands at the top of `/kontakt`, which looks like it
worked and is worse than the 404 it replaced.

Output: six-item navigation at a 1024px desktop breakpoint, two resolved footer shortcuts, a
real `#dojazd` anchor, a first-of-its-kind fragment-resolution gate, and two paths out of
`KNOWN_FUTURE_ROUTES`.

## Slice capability (MVP)

After this plan a parent can find the fees page from the header on any page and on any
device, and every footer shortcut except Galeria lands on real content. Galeria stays pointed
at `/galeria` until plan 05-07 creates `#galeria`, because a repointed link that lands
nowhere is exactly the defect this plan exists to prevent.

**Depends on 05-01** because Amendment v1.7 is what authorizes changing a LOCKED contract's
nav count and mobile breakpoint, and D-20 requires the amendment before the change.
**Depends on 05-02** because `'/cennik'` may only leave `KNOWN_FUTURE_ROUTES` once the route
resolves.
</objective>
