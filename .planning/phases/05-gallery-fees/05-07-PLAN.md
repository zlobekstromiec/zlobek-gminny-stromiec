<objective>
Put the gallery on the page a parent actually reads, and complete the move of the photos out
of `o-nas.json`.

Purpose: GALLERY-01. `/o-nas` is the emptiest page on the site and the one that has to sell
the żłobek to a parent; that is the whole reason the gallery moved onto it and it is the test
a reviewer should apply to the result. The photos should feel like the existing „Nasze
miejsce" section, just more of them, rather than a different visual idiom.

Output: the `#galeria` section, the completed migration, the resolved footer shortcut and an
empty `KNOWN_FUTURE_ROUTES`.

## Slice capability (MVP)

ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 1, verbatim: *"A visitor can view a photo gallery of the
żłobek."*

After task 2 a parent sees the gallery. After task 3 they can reach it from the footer of any
page. Task 2 is unusually large for one task and cannot be split: removing `obiekt_zdjecia`
from `o-nas.json` is a type error in four modules and an undefined access at prerender, so
the key and every reader of it change in one commit or the repository does not compile.

## What closes here

This plan completes GALLERY-01 and the public half of GALLERY-02. The closing evidence for
GALLERY-02 (an editor's add and remove appearing publicly after a rebuild) is GAL-11 and is
live-only. This phase still does NOT tick CMS-01, CMS-02 or CMS-03 (D-36).

## Copy rules, hard, in every string this plan writes

Including code comments, test names and commit messages: no emoji, no em dashes anywhere (use
commas, colons, parentheses), en dash only inside a numeric range, plain hyphen in day
abbreviations. Icons are Lucide or the bespoke set, never emoji. Polish only, on every
visitor-facing surface.
