<objective>
Give `PowtarzalnaGrupa` move-up and move-down, and prove it on the two screens that already
mount it, before anything new depends on it.

Purpose: this is shared-component work with a real regression surface, and two of the three
mount sites belong to screens this phase otherwise does not touch. `05-RESEARCH.md` C-1
corrects the record: the component is mounted by TWO screens at THREE sites, not four
screens, and `wlasnaRamka` is `true` at exactly one of the three. That one is the o-nas photo
group, which is the branch the gallery needs. A developer reading top-down edits the visually
obvious `{:else}` fieldset branch and stops, shipping a gallery with no reorder controls and
two unrelated screens that gained controls nobody asked for.

Output: the two named actions, the third focus-request variant, the buttons on both branches,
and reorder working with scripting disabled on `/admin/o-nas` photos and `/admin/plan-dnia`
rows.

## Slice capability (MVP)

The user here is the editor. After task 2 an editor can reorder the facility photos on
`/admin/o-nas` and after task 3 the rows of `/admin/plan-dnia`, both with a keyboard and both
with JavaScript switched off. That is a capability that did not exist before, on a live,
staff-reachable panel.

## What this plan deliberately does NOT do

The twelve-photo **cap** (D-23) is NOT built here. `05-RESEARCH.md` slice 2 groups it with
the reorder, but the cap has no consumer until `/admin/galeria` exists, and shipping an
opt-in prop with no caller and no test is untested dead code in a shared component. The
`limit` prop lands in plan 05-06 together with the screen that sets it and the test that
exercises it. The reorder is the risky half (three mount sites, two branches, a focus
selector that excludes buttons by construction) and it is proven here.
