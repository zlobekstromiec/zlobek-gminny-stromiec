## Phase Goal

**As a** rodzic (parent), **I want to** read enrollment information, download the wnioski, and send an enrollment or contact message from the website, **so that** I can reach the żłobek and start the enrollment process without a visit, knowing my data is handled lawfully and never stored.

## MVP Slice (this plan)

**Slice A (Kontakt), plan 2 of 3.** The pipeline proven in Plan 01 gets its human interface: a real, accessible, Polish contact form island that a parent can fill in and submit, with the RODO consent and klauzula that make submitting lawful. Plan 04 mounts it on a route and the capability is complete.

<objective>
Build the reusable form UI kit and the contact form island: the Polish copy and klauzula content module, the accessible field and consent primitives, the Turnstile widget wrapper, and the KontaktForm island that submits to /api/kontakt and handles every result branch truthfully (CONTACT-03, RECRUIT-04, D-03, D-04, D-10, D-11, D-12).

Purpose: forms are this project's highest-risk accessibility surface and its only RODO-bearing interface. Building the primitives once, to the locked UI-SPEC contract, is what keeps both forms compliant and identical.
Output: a content module, three shared components, one working island, and a unit test pinning the copy and klauzula obligations.
</objective>
