## Phase Goal

**As a** rodzic (parent), **I want to** read enrollment information, download the wnioski, and send an enrollment or contact message from the website, **so that** I can reach the żłobek and start the enrollment process without a visit, knowing my data is handled lawfully and never stored.

## MVP Slice (this plan)

**Slice A (Kontakt), plan 3 of 3.** This plan closes the first vertical slice of the phase: after it, a parent can open /kontakt in a browser, see how to reach the żłobek, and send a message that arrives as e-mail. The hero's "Zadzwoń do nas" call to action and the navigation link to /kontakt stop being known-future 404s.

<objective>
Compose and ship the /kontakt route: contact cards, the map panel, the contact form island and the Urzad Gminy information box, then enable the route in the prerender crawler and the sitemap and lock the whole thing behind an acceptance and accessibility spec (CONTACT-01, CONTACT-02, CONTACT-03, D-16, D-17).

Purpose: turn the proven endpoint and the proven island into a page a parent can actually use.
Output: a live prerendered /kontakt route, an enforced link target, and a green per-route Playwright plus axe suite.
</objective>
