<objective>
Close the Blocker gap from 04-VERIFICATION.md (CR-01) and the unremediated Warning WR-01, both in `src/lib/server/forms/ratelimit.ts`.

CR-01: the limiter passes `expirationTtl` on every accepted write. Cloudflare KV treats that value as absolute-from-now, so each write restarts the clock. The site-wide single-key daily counter `rl:doba` therefore never expires while traffic keeps arriving: it climbs monotonically to 40 and then returns 429 to every parent on BOTH forms until a full 24 h of total site silence passes, cyclically. On a deliberately no-storage pipeline the blocked enquiry leaves no record anywhere. That is a self-inflicted denial of service against genuine enrollment enquiries, the exact opposite of ROADMAP Success Criterion 3 ("with the endpoint rate-limiting abuse").

WR-01: an absent or blank `RATE_LIMIT_SALT` falls through to an unsalted SHA-256 of the client IP. The IPv4 space is small enough to enumerate, so the stored key stops being one-way and becomes a reversible pseudonym of the visitor, contradicting the klauzula informacyjna sentence that `tests/forms-copy.unit.ts` pins as required RODO copy. The salt IS provisioned in production today (04-07), so this is not currently exploitable; the defect is that nothing guards the degraded path if the secret is ever unset or badly rotated.

Purpose: make the two windows genuinely fixed by putting the WINDOW IN THE KEY (calendar date for the daily ceiling, hour-of-epoch for the per-client ceiling) and demoting `expirationTtl` to a cleanup-only lifetime, then refuse to hash without a salt. Both fixes preserve the documented fail-open degrade policy.

Output: a corrected limiter, a rate-limit unit block rewritten in lockstep (the current suite pins the buggy behaviour and goes RED the moment the bug is fixed), refreshed operator docs, and a FORM-02 requirement mark that tells the truth until the live re-check is done.
</objective>
