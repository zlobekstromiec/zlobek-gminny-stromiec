## Phase Goal

**As a** rodzic (parent), **I want to** read enrollment information, download the wnioski, and send an enrollment or contact message from the website, **so that** I can reach the żłobek and start the enrollment process without a visit, knowing my data is handled lawfully and never stored.

## MVP Slice (this plan)

**Slice B (Rekrutacja), plan 2 of 2.** This plan closes the second and last user-facing slice of the phase: a parent can open /rekrutacja, learn where they stand, see how points are awarded, download the wniosek, understand that it is filed in person at the Urząd Gminy, and leave their details for the waiting list. Every navigation link in the site then resolves.

<objective>
Compose and ship the /rekrutacja route from the confirmed regulamin content: status banner, zgloszenie form, criteria and points table, the real procedure, the compact fee box and the wnioski download list, then enable the route in the crawler and the sitemap and lock it behind an acceptance and accessibility spec (RECRUIT-01, RECRUIT-02, RECRUIT-05, D-01, D-06, D-14, D-15).

Purpose: this is the page the żłobek's audience actually comes for, and it is the page where publishing a wrong fact does the most damage. Its content is authored directly from the regulamin digest, under the source document's do-not-publish gate.
Output: a live prerendered /rekrutacja route, an enforced link target, and a green per-route Playwright plus axe suite.
</objective>
