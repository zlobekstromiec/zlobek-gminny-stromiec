# Quick 261024-sq8: director's badge logo on every brand surface Summary

The director's circular elephant badge replaces the temporary sun-and-house artwork in the public header (52 px), the public footer (128 px, directly on brand-blue), the panel bar (40 px), the panel login (52 px) and both favicons, generated from `pictures/Logo żłobek.png` by an edge-seeded flood fill knockout, with the two retired PNGs git-removed and UI-SPEC Amendment v1.9 recording the new single-asset model.

| `static/favicon.png` | 512 x 512 | 119,893 | RGBA palette PNG, transparent canvas, badge fit to 512 (was 157,463 before) |

## Generator approach

Throwaway ESM script `gen-badge.mjs` in the session scratchpad (not in the repo). It chdirs into the repo, imports sharp 0.35.2 by absolute path, decodes the source with `ensureAlpha().raw()`, runs an iterative flood fill seeded from every border pixel (background when R, G and B are all at least 245), and clears alpha only on visited pixels. Result: 1,965,587 pixels cleared, 278 enclosed near-white highlights kept opaque, alpha bbox x 160 to 1837, y 96 to 1778 (matches the planner probe exactly), cropped with 4 px padding to 1686 x 1691, then resized to a 1024 longest side with lanczos3. Favicons are resized first and composited second (sharp resize-before-composite trap). No feather. Every output and both composites (on `#0369A1` and on white) plus 32 px and 16 px favicon downscales were opened and inspected: ring complete, both ribbon tails present, title and ribbon text uncut, no white halo on blue. At 16 px the favicon reads as a purple disc with a teal rim and a pale elephant blob, which is the accepted outcome.

## Footer decision

No card. The badge sits directly on brand-blue at a fixed 128 px width in `.brand-badge`, left-aligned with the column text. The live screenshots at 390 px and 1280 px confirmed it reads cleanly, so the optional card restore was not used. Recorded in Amendment v1.9 §3.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Performance] Favicons encoded as palette PNGs**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The painterly texture of the badge defeats lossless PNG: the 512 favicon came out at 436 KB against 157 KB before, and `static/favicon.png` is served raw to every visitor, unlike the enhanced:img asset.
- **Fix:** `png({ palette: true, quality: 100, effort: 10, compressionLevel: 9 })` for both favicons. Favicon 120 KB, apple icon 14 KB (was 38 KB lossless, 21 KB before). Re-inspected after re-encode; no visible difference. Transparency of the favicon and opacity of the apple icon both asserted.
- **Files modified:** static/favicon.png, static/apple-touch-icon.png
- **Commit:** a386a75
